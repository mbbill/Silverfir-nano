The engineer's own qualitative assessment of the unified dynamic register budget
that replaced the static split: it gives "slightly better results but much more
complicated code," and he is explicitly unsure it is actually the best solution —
the choice is held at LOW confidence and the question is "answered-but-contested"
rather than settled. ALGORITHM4 is described as the *fourth* iteration of the
budget-splitting algorithm, "far from perfect, with big room for improvement," a
replaceable implementation rather than an invariant.

This is a judgment, not a benchmark — and that is exactly its significance. The
measured facts (better results) point one way; the deciding fact (maintenance /
complexity cost) is real but unmeasured. It is the canonical "measurement gap" case
the design-representation work flagged: the value function only guides as well as
its facts are complete, and the decisive fact here is qualitative.
